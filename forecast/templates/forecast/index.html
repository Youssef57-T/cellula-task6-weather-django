Pick a start time and press Forecast

The app takes the recorded temperatures before the start time and forecasts the hours after it. Choose any hour from 2001 to the end of 2026. For hours up to the end of 2025 the forecast is drawn next to what was actually recorded, so you can see how well the model did. The data ends there, so 2026 forecasts get less reliable the further they are from it.

  • SARIMA was the most accurate model in Task 5.
  • Enter your own latest reading to see how the forecast reacts.
  • Every forecast is also available as JSON at /api/forecast/.
